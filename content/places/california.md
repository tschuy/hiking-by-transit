California's natural landscapes are famous, and from the redwood forests to the shoreline waters this is for good reason. The state has a wealth of unique and bold flora and fauna across bold landforms.

Whether you want to go for a hike in your backyard, take a bus into the heart of Yosemite National Park, or go for a multi-day backpacking trip, California's public lands are open and accessible.

The Valley of Heart's Delight was once famous for its fresh fruit, flowering trees, and scenic beauty. Today, the orchards are mostly gone, but the hills remain above the traffic and development.

The South Bay is a large region, with the San José basin stretching roughly 15 miles from side to side. This makes getting beyond the urban area more difficult than in other parts of the Bay, and some transit-accessible hiking is served only on weekdays. Even so, all-week routes provide access to foothill preserves, creek corridors, historic ranchland, and panoramic views.

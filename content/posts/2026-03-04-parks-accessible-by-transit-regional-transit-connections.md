title: "Parks accessible by transit will be on the Regional Transit Connections map"
---

In March 2026, Bay Area's new Regional Transit Connections map was unveiled. Thanks to advocacy from Hiking by Transit, the map includes parks accessible in the 9-county Bay Area. Read more about the new map on the Seamless Bay Area blog: <a href="https://www.seamlessbayarea.org/blog/2026/3/4/victory-parks-accessible-by-transit-will-be-on-the-regional-transit-connections-map">Parks accessible by transit will be on the Regional Transit Connections map</a>.
